{{- /* A page's date in the blog's time zone (US Central; params.time_zone overrides it), for filing posts by day, month and year. micro.blog hands Hugo every date at a fixed -05:00 offset, winter and summer (live 2026-10-24: December posts carry -05:00), while a post's URL and its year section follow Central time. So a winter post written after 11 PM showed under the next day, month or year: /2025/ opened with a January holding the Airport Rally post of 2025/12/31, and 75 posts sat on the wrong On This Day. Returns a time.Time. time.In reads the server's zone files (Hugo's release builds don't embed them), so where it can't, this keeps micro.blog's date rather than failing the build; check-live's year-page and On This Day rules would then fail. */ -}} {{- $date := .Date -}} {{- with try (time.In (site.Params.time_zone | default "America/Chicago") .Date) -}} {{- if not .Err }}{{ $date = .Value }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $date -}}
