{{- /* A smaller copy of one of Jamie's uploaded photos, for thumbnails. Usage: {{ partial "photo-src.html" (dict "src" $url "size" 400 ["square" true]) }} micro.blog's resizer has two forms (tried live 2026-10-21): https://micro.blog/photos/x/ scales to that width and keeps the photo's shape; https://micro.blog/photos// also crops a square, as wide as the scaled photo's shorter side.
